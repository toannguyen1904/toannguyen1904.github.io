I am a CS Ph.D. student at the University of Southern California, started in Fall 2025. I am co-advised by Prof. Yue Wang and Prof. Daniel Seita. Prior to joining USC, I was an AI Resident at the FPT AI Center. I graduated from Ho Chi Minh City University of Science.

Nowadays, I'm particularly interested in:
  • Humanoid loco-manipulation: Developing whole-body loco-manipulation policies that exploit the humanoid form factor to perform challenging tasks in diverse environments.
  • Long-context robot learning: Enabling in-context imitation learning and robots that can reason, track progress, and self-correct in complex and long-horizon tasks.
  • Robot Safety: Robots that work safely and reliably alongside humans in unstructured open-world environments.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to discuss ideas or explore collaboration opportunities!
